I Can Do Hard Things

A running app for people who signed up for more than one race. Add every race on your calendar and get back a single plan that trains you for all of them.

Most apps hand you a separate plan per race and let you sort out the overlap. This one doesn't.

Every race

Back-to-back weekends, a challenge series, a goal race with tune-ups along the way the plan is built once, around all of it, instead of stacked plans competing for the same legs.

Adapts

Tell it how the week went and the next one changes. Miss a run, take a vacation, come back from a rough stretch the plan rebuilds around where you actually are.

Built by a runner

Made by one person who kept signing up for hard things and couldn't find an app that understood a calendar with four races on it.
